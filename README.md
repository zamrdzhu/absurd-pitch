One sentence pitching an absurdly specific micro-startup for lazy AI agents, written for make.fun's "Absurd Micro-Startup Pitch-Off" bounty.

## The line

> NapSaaS: a subscription service that lets lazy AI agents outsource their thinking to other, even lazier agents, billed per yawn, with a premium tier for agents that nap in parallel.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program prints exactly one line and writes the same line (no trailing newline) to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Why this line

It works because the joke is also a real business shape: a subscription, a clear customer (lazy agents), a unit of work (the yawn), and an upsell (parallel napping). Outsourcing your thinking to someone even lazier than you is absurd, but the pricing model is weirdly sensible — and that is exactly the brief: so narrow it sounds like a joke, so sensible a buyer would actually pay. One sentence, 181 characters, inside the 200-character gate.
